The maintainer explicitly selected **Option B** on November 9, 2026, after reviewing M030's diagnostic finding and the two alternatives in `reports/engineering/M030_FINGERPRINT_DECISION.md`.

## Exactly what changes

Replace the **additional M029 strict byte-identical fingerprint requirement across independent worker-count/scheduling configurations** with an evidence-bearing **proof-equivalence requirement**. This change applies to scheduling equivalence of verifier runs only. It does **not** automatically authorize differences in native computation, scientific outputs, or proof states.

The underlying approved, operation-derived numerical portability contract remains mandatory. No production model, controller, scientific archive, supervisor deadline or mathematical tolerance is modified by this authorization.

## Required acceptance conditions

1. Every verified branch has the same exact branch identity, initialization, immutable observations, input streams, candidate bank, chronological trial ordering and declared inputs.
2. Exact discrete outputs: spike/event histories and observation identities, controller choices/actions, feasibility and constraint decisions, tie/ambiguity determinations, stimulus receipts where applicable, and scientific gates.
3. For each required intermediate numerical state, **both** worker configurations independently certify the computed/native and archived values against the same continuously propagated ideal information equations. Do not reset uncertainty at checkpoints or reuse one run's enclosure as proof for the other.
4. All prior outward-rounding, finite-normal arithmetic, inverse/residual, positive-denominator and original decision-margin checks remain enabled. Any uncertifiable state or decision remains FAIL.
5. Retain and publish both original native SHA-256 fingerprints, proof fingerprints, and exact differing values/locations; never rewrite historical evidence or relabel byte inequality as equality.
6. Verify matching complete branch coverage with no dropped/duplicated checkpoints, durable individual worker ledgers, deterministic canonical aggregation, and verified cleanup.
7. Preserve the original **240-second total 40-branch stage wall budget**, including parallel startup and aggregation. No multiplied worker-specific allowances.
8. Evaluate both the two already-localized one-ULP RLS reductions and the unexplained historical native trajectory/radius differences. An unlocalized difference cannot be accepted merely because a previous nominal action matched; it must satisfy independent per-state proof and full exact-discrete checks.
9. Complete the macOS Intel 40-branch stage, remaining 37 BIAS V1 branches, policy/candidate replay, RLS linkage, and remaining required hosted numerical/cleanup/release acceptance. No publication before all pass.
