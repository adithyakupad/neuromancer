## FIR representation and initialization

Let k denote a 20 ms interval [t_k,t_k+20000us); u_k is its delivered four-current vector, y_k its completed raw four-sector rate. Predict y_k using x_k=[u_k,u_(k−1),...,u_(k−9)]. Current u_k is planned before interval execution; current y_k is never available to its own forecast. All actual model updates use RECEIPT-confirmed inputs, never requested or undelivered plans. Planner parameters are frozen within each prospective plan.

At absolute51 s fit the selected FIR family, q=10, lambda=.1, on exactly k=800..2349 ([16,47) s), using raw sector rates and completed inputs. x history is available through k−9. Compute fit-only mu, scale=max(population SD,1 pA), output mean ybar. z=[1,(x−mu)/scale]. Theta0 solves (Z'Z+n*.1D)Theta0=Z'(Y−ybar), D=diag(0,1,...,1). P0 is inverse information. Use float64 and fixed normalization/output center for the whole control episode. Input/output units and lag order match M004; raw labels and fitting window are explicitly changed. No historical coefficients or efficacy supplied. Four-output coefficients: 41*4=164, covariance 41*41; deployed scalar prediction is their equal mean.

Original prefix has u0=u2 and u1=u3=0. Full 41-column rank is impossible; only C02 responses can be identified. Require rank of reduced standardized [1,q_k,...,q_(k−9)] =11 and condition <=1e6. Record all singular values and full rank; do not describe ridge as restoring independent channel identification. Full penalized information condition <=1e12, positive definiteness, finite parameters required. A failed initialization is a trial failure, retained with safe ramp; no per-network model replacement or exclusion. The original calibration validity remains a separate requirement.

Models:
- FIXED-MPC: Theta0 unchanged. Same completed data accessible but no parameter updates.
- RLS-MPC: same Theta0/P0, rho=.999 per completed 20 ms observation, exactly M004 RLS999. K=Pz/(rho+z'Pz), Theta+=K((y−ybar)−z'Theta), P+=(P−Kz'P)/rho, symmetrized. Discounted initial ridge, not repeated constant-penalty ridge. No true gain, change detector trigger or reset. 3,200 possible updates per episode, first after first interval; first plan is shared with FIXED-MPC. Same checks as M004 plus finite forecast checks and transaction-before-commit on information failure. Check eigenvalues/condition at first and every50th receipt; denominator positive, min eigen>=−1e−8, condition<=1e12. Failure latches, records cause, then safe ramp; no silent PI substitute.
- BIAS-MPC diagnostic: same planner and initial full coefficients, freeze all input coefficients; update only four intercepts by scalar RLS rho=.999, P_b0=1/n, target=y−ybar−z_inputs'Theta_inputs. This is a conditional baseline-update ablation, not full-RLS equivalence. It receives identical data. If RLS and BIAS perform similarly, do not claim kernel adaptation is necessary.

## Finite-bank receding-horizon planner

Predictive methods share ALL planner settings and their initial model; only update policy differs. Control decision at each completed integer-second boundary; first decision before relative0. Horizon H=2 s=100 bins, two 1 s desired holds q0,q1. Each q is in G={0,5,...,120} pA: 25^2=625 candidates. Generate EXACT delivered ramp for each candidate from the actual last delivered vector using unchanged constraints; preserve the actual last nine input vectors. Restrict to C02 states; a non-C02 prior rejects planning and safe-ramps rather than inventing unmeasured channel effects.

For each candidate predict all100 bin outputs by the FIR, then two raw one-second means yhat_h. Minimize

J = sum_(h=0,1) (yhat_h−r)^2 + .02 * sum_(k=0..99) .02*(q_k/120)^2 + .01 * sum_(h=0,1) ((q_h−q_(h−1))/120)^2,

where q_(−1) is actual current before the origin, q_k is DELIVERED scalar current and q_h is requested hold. First term units Hz²; .02 and .01 have Hz² scaling, duration normalized implicitly by 1 s. No terminal bonus/constraint, no optimizer lookahead gain schedule, no uncertainty interval tightening. The cost trades tracking against modest normalized current/switching; no outcome-based cost tuning. Log predicted individual terms and selected objective. Ties within1e−12 choose lower delivered exposure, then smaller q0 then q1. Enumerate all625 exactly, bounded vectorized arithmetic; this is a finite-bank optimum, NOT a continuous-action or neural-optimality claim. Apply ONLY the first1 s plan, respecting per-tick delivery and safety interruption; discard the second plan when replan occurs. Do not assimilate hypothetical plan outcomes.

Planning computation limit .25 s suspend-inclusive per decision, including schedule/feature/cost construction and checks, plus <=625 candidates. Check before/after stages and before action release. Expiration discards the decision; safe-ramp toward zero at next legal tick and latch model/planner failure. Virtual time does not advance during computation; record wall latency separately and never claim real-time hardware qualification. The .25 s cap is smaller than1 s cadence, yet OS scheduling can exceed it; fail closed rather than backdate a decision. No numerical QP solver/dependency or indefinite optimization is needed.

Reject malformed histories, nonfinite coefficients/targets, candidate nonfinite or |sector forecast|>100 Hz. If ANY candidate is invalid, mark planning failure rather than silently selecting a favorable surviving subset. Negative predicted rates are logged (linear approximation), not clipped. Record minimum forecast; persistent negative predictions warn of mismatch, while magnitude cap/numerical checks are mandatory. No gain-shaped model constraints tuned from evaluator labels. A valid but wrong model can choose poor actions; safety constraints bound current, NOT neural tracking performance or stability.

## Controller message contracts and missing data

Public inputs: completed permitted Observation (trial ID,start/stop/available_us,counts,mask,coverage), Receipt (requested/delivered command/times/status), known constant target and immutable constraints; private controller/model state. No engine, gain, voltage, connectivity, evaluator population or future Observation field. API: plan(origin_us,last9_delivered,last_current,model_snapshot,target)->625-plan diagnostics/first command; accept(completed_observation,receipt,now_us)->next private model/policy state. Origin must equal completed public clock. Receipt/observation pairing, trial identity, mask/coverage and integer grid validated.

At every20ms tick: deliver previously chosen legal command; advance neural dynamics; expose completed observation; record pre-update prediction; assimilate only a valid completed observation; at each1s decision boundary use latest available model to plan next second. PI consumes its original50-bin raw aggregate. No current-bin outcome influences that current's action. Planner may receive future actions it constructs, never future actual deliveries assumed to have happened. All parameter updates are after corresponding forecast/observation availability.

Primary timely data: zero missing/delay/fallback expected. Secondary faults: at the due tick, late/missing/invalid observation triggers next-tick nearest-zero ramp, clears aggregation, freezes updates, then four consecutive fresh valid bins rearm as original Policy; stale late observations are logged/discarded, never replayed into a later model state. RLS never uses missing/recovery bins; it reopens updates after rearm. Planning resumes at the next fixed1s boundary with last9 actual inputs and valid model; PI integral preserved/frozen per original behavior. Numerical/planner failure instead latches zero for rest of trial. Fallback events are outcomes, never replaced by successful-arm metrics. A future runner must process per-tick safety even inside a planned1s hold; blindly precomputing and executing the whole second under injected faults is prohibited. Normal no-fault output must reproduce original PI exactly.
