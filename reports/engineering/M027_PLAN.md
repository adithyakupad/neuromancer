## Soundness and performance

Bounds must be operation-derived with explicit finite/normal arithmetic preconditions, directed rounding, and conditioning checks. Higher-precision deterministic tests are independent of production NumPy evaluation. Small-matrix gain, covariance, coefficient, prediction, objective, tie, zero/subnormal and denominator cases are required. Exact action identity alone is insufficient. No uncertainty may be reset to an archived checkpoint.

Measure original and proposed arithmetic on identical inputs. Separate interval work, native state updates, planner bounds, archive decoding, state digests, persistence and supervisor overhead. Cache only immutable input-derived quantities. Preserve all samples, source hashes, versions, thread requests versus observed behavior, and peak RSS. A faster prerequisite is not a full certificate or a scientific result.

If any mathematical or resource gate fails, preserve HOLD and report the smallest remaining blocker without extending this campaign. All original exact-byte failures and negative scientific findings remain historical evidence.
